Photon Fusion Physics Addon - Release History

## 2.1.1

**Changes**

- Changed obsolete usages of Physics.autoSimulation.

## 2.1.0

**What's New**

- Expose PhysicsBody in NetworkRigidbody that allows unified interactions for both Rigidbody and Rigidbody2D.

**Changes**

- NetworkRigidbody now warns and self-despawns if no Rigidbody or Rigidbody2D component is found on the object.

**Bug Fixes**

- Teleport handling now discards the correct snapshot and snaps to the teleport location.
- Forcing awake of NetworkRigidbodies on spawned for initial sync.
- Spawn position of NetworkRigidbody on clients has been fixed.
